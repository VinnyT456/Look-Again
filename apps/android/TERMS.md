# Look Again Terms of Use (Android)

Last updated October 15, 2026.

These terms cover your use of Look Again. Google Play's terms also apply when you get it from Google Play.

## What Look Again does

It estimates whether a photo looks real, edited or AI-generated, using a detection model that runs on your device. It's a tool to help you decide what to trust, not a final answer.

## Verdicts are estimates

No detector is always right. A verdict can be wrong in either direction, and the confidence number is the model's own score, not a guarantee. Don't rely on a verdict alone for anything important, such as news, legal, academic or safety decisions.

## Use it fairly

Don't use a verdict to harass, shame or publicly accuse someone of faking a photo. If something matters, look for other evidence: the original poster, other photos of the same moment, and trusted reporting. Don't use the app to break the law or anyone's rights.
