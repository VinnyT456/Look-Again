# WildcatVerify Privacy Policy (web and browser extension)

WildcatVerify is built so your photos and checks stay private. Last updated October 15, 2026.

## Photos you choose

Your browser gives the site only the photo you choose, drop or paste. It is sent to the WildcatVerify server to be checked, held in memory there only while it is analyzed, and never written to disk or kept. In your browser it stays in memory and is discarded when you delete the check, clear My Checks, or close the tab.

## Bluesky links you paste

To check a post, the WildcatVerify server asks Bluesky's public API for that post's text and photo. No account is used, and the server keeps no copy of the link. See the [Bluesky Privacy Policy](https://bsky.social/about/support/privacy-policy).

## The Feed

The Feed loads public image posts from Bluesky's What's Hot feed. Images and avatars load straight from Bluesky's servers, which see your IP address like any website would. Posts are only checked when you choose them.

## The browser extension

When you check an image or post from the extension, only that image or link is sent to the WildcatVerify server, checked the same way, and discarded when you choose Done.

The extension can read pages only on bsky.app, and only to see which post is open in the current tab; it never reads the rest of the page. It keeps two settings in your browser (the server address and whether to show where the detector looked). Open in My Checks passes that one result to the WildcatVerify web app in your browser; the photo itself is not included when you uploaded it.

## My Checks

By default, past checks are kept in memory and disappear when you close the tab. If you turn on Settings → Keep My Checks, results and small copies of the photos you checked are saved in your browser only, never uploaded, and deleted when you turn the setting off or erase your data.

## Settings and score

Your preferences and your best Look Again score are saved in your browser only. Erase All Data removes them.

## What WildcatVerify doesn't do

- No account or sign-in
- No analytics, ads or tracking
- No data sold, shared or collected

## Your choices

Delete one check, clear My Checks, or use Settings → Erase All Data. Clearing the site's data in your browser removes everything. The developer holds no personal data, but you can still [contact us](https://github.com/VinnyT456/Look-Again/issues) with any privacy request.
