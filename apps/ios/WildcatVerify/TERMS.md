## What WildcatVerify does

WildcatVerify estimates whether a photo looks real, edited or AI-generated, using a
detection model that runs on your iPhone. It is a tool to help you decide what to trust,
not a final answer.

## Verdicts are estimates

No detector is always right. A verdict can be wrong in either direction, and the confidence
number is the model's own score, not a guarantee. Don't rely on a verdict alone for anything
important, such as news, legal, academic or safety decisions.

## Use it fairly

Don't use a verdict to harass, shame or publicly accuse someone of faking a photo. If
something matters, look for other evidence: the original poster, other photos of the same
moment, and trusted reporting. Don't use the app to break the law or anyone's rights.
